📌 ADVANCE BOOKING / ACTION CHECKLIST — DO THESE IN ORDER

  • Before departure: Check all 4 Singapore passports are valid for the whole Korea stay. For this trip, Singapore passport holders can enter Korea visa-free for up to 90 days and are temporarily exempt from K-ETA through 31 Dec 2026. Do not pay for K-ETA unless you deliberately choose to apply for one.
  • 27–29 Nov (within 3 days before arrival): Submit the Korea e-Arrival Card for the family. It is free. Prepare each passport, an email address, arrival date + flight number, first Korea accommodation details (Hotel The Botanik Sewoon Myeongdong, 23-1 Eulji-ro 19-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul), and departure date/flight number if available. One mobile submission can cover up to 9 people. Official e-Arrival Card. Save the issue number/email confirmation; no paper arrival card is needed after electronic submission.
  • Customs on arrival: If carrying anything that must be declared, complete the required customs/personal-effects declaration and use the declaration channel. If nothing is declarable, follow the airport's Nothing to Declare process. Recheck Korea Customs shortly before departure if bringing large amounts of cash, restricted food/medicines or high-value goods. Korea Customs passenger guide.
